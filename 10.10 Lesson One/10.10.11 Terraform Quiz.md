# 10.10.11 Terraform Quiz

#### Getting Help from `terraform` CLI

There's so many ways to get help from the `terraform` command line!

* `terraform -help init`
* `terraform init -h`
* `terraform --h init`

### How to Download Community Plugins?

Use `terraform init`, it will download provider plugins referenced in your terraform config.
